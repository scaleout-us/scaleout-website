ScaleOut LLC Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026 Last Updated: October 24, 2026

ScaleOut LLC (“ScaleOut,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a Georgia limited liability company, provides website assessments, technical evaluations, and related digital services. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you interact with our advertisements (including on Nextdoor and other ad platforms), visit our website, request a PHP website vulnerability assessment, or communicate with us.

1 Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you, automatically through your device when visiting our digital properties, and from public website sources:

  • Contact and Account Information: Name, email address, telephone number, and communication preferences.
  • Website and Infrastructure Details: Website URL, domain name, hosting provider, and related infrastructure details you provide.
  • Temporary Login Credentials: If your website’s PHP version or security posture cannot be evaluated through publicly accessible endpoints, you may choose to provide temporary, limited administrative or hosting credentials. Providing credentials is strictly voluntary.
  • Publicly Accessible Technical Data: Data collected from public DNS records, HTTP response headers, visible source files, public directory structures, and related open web findings.
  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: When you visit our website or landing pages, we and our third-party partners use cookies, web beacons, and tracking pixels (such as the Nextdoor Pixel, Google, or Meta pixels) to collect technical data (IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed) for performance analytics and ad retargeting.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Perform the requested PHP website vulnerability assessment.
  • Deliver assessment findings and security recommendations through secure channels.
  • Communicate with you regarding technical questions, follow-ups, and customer support.
  • Market ScaleOut LLC services to you via direct email, manual phone calls, text messages (SMS), and online retargeting advertisements across third-party platforms.
  • Measure advertising effectiveness, track marketing attribution, and improve our services.
  • Prevent fraud, comply with applicable laws, and maintain business records.

Important: We never use temporary login credentials or non-public data obtained through privileged access for marketing or advertising purposes.

3 Login Credentials and Privileged Access

Where non-public access is required to determine your PHP environment or vulnerability status:

  • Minimum Access Principle: We request only the level of access necessary to perform the assessment. We encourage clients to provision a temporary, isolated user account rather than sharing primary administrative credentials.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Login credentials are used exclusively by ScaleOut personnel to conduct the requested technical evaluation and are never transferred, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • Credential Deletion: All credentials provided to us are deleted immediately upon completion of the assessment, or earlier upon your request. We advise you to revoke or change any shared credentials once the evaluation is finished.

4 Assessment Data and Retention Policies

We distinguish between publicly accessible data and privileged findings:

  • Privileged Assessment Findings: Any scan results, configuration reports, notes, or data collected through privileged access will be delivered to you via secure means. ScaleOut will permanently delete its copies of privileged assessment findings within 30 days of completion or immediately upon your written request.
  • Public Website Information: We may retain publicly observable information regarding your website (such as open headers, domain records, and general tech stack details) indefinitely for historical context, business operations, and service development.
  • Lead and Contact Information: We retain contact details (name, email, phone number, website URL, hosting provider) and marketing history indefinitely to maintain our business relationship with you, manage marketing communications, and track service history, unless you request deletion or opt out of our marketing communications.

5 Cookies, Retargeting, and Advertising Pixels

We use first- and third-party cookies and tracking pixels on our website and landing pages:

  • Advertising and Retargeting: Tracking pixels collect information about your interaction with our site and share it with advertising networks (e.g., Nextdoor, Google, Meta). These networks use this data to display relevant ScaleOut advertisements to you on other websites and platforms.
  • Opting Out of Tracking: You can configure your browser to block or alert you about cookies. To opt out of interest-based advertising, you may visit industry opt-out portals such as the Digital Advertising Alliance or the Network Advertising Initiative.

6 Marketing Communications and Your Choices

By submitting your contact information, you agree to receive marketing communications from ScaleOut LLC:

  • Email: We send promotional and informational emails. Every marketing email includes a working unsubscribe link. You may opt out at any time, and we honor opt-out requests promptly in compliance with the CAN-SPAM Act.
  • Phone Calls: We may reach out via manual phone calls from our team. We do not use automated robocalls or pre-recorded telemarketing messages. You may request to be placed on our internal do-not-call list at any time.
  • Text Messages (SMS): We may send transactional and promotional text messages manually to the phone number you provide. Standard message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP, or ask for help by replying HELP.
  • Transactional Notices: Opting out of marketing communications does not affect operational or service-related messages regarding an active vulnerability assessment you requested.

7 Service Providers and Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information with reputable, vetted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our business under confidentiality standards, including:

  • Cloud infrastructure and secure hosting providers.
  • Secure credential management and encrypted communication tools.
  • Email delivery, CRM, and calendar scheduling platforms.
  • Website analytics and advertising networks (for tracking and retargeting).
  • Professional advisors (legal, accounting, and compliance).

We may also disclose information where required by law, subpoena, or regulation, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of ScaleOut LLC or our clients.

8 Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information and assessment data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no internet transmission or digital storage system is guaranteed to be completely impenetrable. We recommend using encrypted channels when transmitting sensitive technical information.

9 Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we maintain about you.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request the deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal and record-keeping requirements.
  • Objection and Restriction: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or restrict certain uses.

You can exercise these rights, or opt out of marketing communications, by contacting us directly using the information provided in Section 13.

10 Children’s Privacy

Our services and this website are intended for business owners, technical administrators, and professionals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we will take immediate steps to delete it.

11 Third-Party Links and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites or third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site before providing personal information.

12 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective upon posting to this page with an updated “Last Updated” date. By continuing to access our services after changes become effective, you accept the revised policy. If you object to the changes, please contact us to opt out of further marketing or to request deletion of your retained data.
